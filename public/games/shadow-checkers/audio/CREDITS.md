# Shadow Checkers study audio

- Music remains the original Shadow Checkers performance, `heart-of-courage.mp3`, played quietly. The September 24 mobile delivery copy is stereo MP3 at 128 kb/s; its full duration, tempo and pitch are preserved. The exact prior file is retained as `ArtSource/Audio/heart-of-courage-master.mp3`, with the reproducible delivery encoding in `prepare_study_contacts.ps1`.
- `fireplace-loop.mp3`: derived from **Dry grass burning in open fireplace**, recorded by **ezwa**, June 17, 2007, released into the public domain by the author. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dry_grass_burning_in_open_fireplace.ogg . Edited to a 23-second crossfaded loop, filtered and transcoded to mono VBR for quiet background playback. Original recording and authored loop master retained in the Unity project's ArtSource/Audio; delivery encoding is reproducible with `ArtSource/prepare_hearth_audio.ps1`.
- `stone-place-0.mp3` through `stone-place-3.mp3`: derived from **Impact Sounds 1.0**, **Kenney**, CC0. Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds ; license: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . September 24 revision replaces the pitched plate impacts with four lightly blended wood/glass contacts, softened attacks, restrained high frequencies and short natural decays. Peak limiting preserves headroom; runtime avoids heavy pitch-down. Original sources and the reproducible `prepare_study_contacts.ps1` recipe are in the Unity project's ArtSource folder.
- `stone-slide.mp3`: filtered and softened adaptation of the existing ShadowChat `notification-sounds/checkers_move.wav`, preserving its original source file.

Runtime adds small gain and playback-rate variations, avoids consecutive identical placement recordings, and sometimes omits the slide for a clean lifted placement. Fire and pieces follow the shared game-SFX preference; the soundtrack follows the separate existing game-music preference.
