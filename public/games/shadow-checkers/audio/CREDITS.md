# Shadow Checkers study audio

- Music remains the existing original Shadow Checkers soundtrack, `heart-of-courage.mp3`. This work changes its playback level only; it does not replace or modify the recording.
- `fireplace-loop.mp3`: derived from **Dry grass burning in open fireplace**, recorded by **ezwa**, June 17, 2007, released into the public domain by the author. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dry_grass_burning_in_open_fireplace.ogg . Edited to a crossfaded loop, filtered and transcoded for quiet background playback. Original retained in the Unity project's ArtSource/Audio.
- `stone-place-0.mp3` through `stone-place-3.mp3`: derived from **Impact Sounds 1.0**, **Kenney**, CC0. Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds ; license: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Light plate contact recordings were lowered in pitch, filtered and shortened for marble-token placement.
- `stone-slide.mp3`: filtered and softened adaptation of the existing ShadowChat `notification-sounds/checkers_move.wav`, preserving its original source file.

Runtime adds small gain and playback-rate variations, avoids consecutive identical placement recordings, and sometimes omits the slide for a clean lifted placement. Fire and pieces follow the shared game-SFX preference; the soundtrack follows the separate existing game-music preference.
