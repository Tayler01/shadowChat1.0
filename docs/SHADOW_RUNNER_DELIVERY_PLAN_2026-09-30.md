# Shadow Runner Delivery Reset

## Authority

September 30: Tayler identified excessive elapsed time and explicitly requested
diagnosis and correction of the inefficient workflow. This plan changes delivery
and verification practices, not the authorized game scope or earned progress.
It supersedes the blanket 44-replay prerequisite in earlier rebuild documents.
The exhaustive rebuild goal remains active; a phone-test release is a milestone,
not automatic completion of outstanding art, story, or device validation.

## What Went Wrong

- Automated route strategy development became a second product. A bot stall was
  repeatedly followed by input-policy refinement and another long playthrough,
  even when a game defect had not been demonstrated.
- A mandatory 44-cell normal/mastery/browser matrix repeatedly invalidated useful
  prior evidence after changes that did not affect every cell.
- Large overlapping checklists mixed critical defects, creative aspirations,
  tooling improvements, and physical-device evidence into one stopping condition.
- Late integration with main created avoidable conflicts and fresh regression work.
- Forecasts extrapolated that expanding checklist rather than a bounded release.
- Current evidence: 85 changed/new verification files, 42 production-source
  files, six documents and 208 local Runner evidence directories. These are
  file counts, not a measured breakdown of elapsed engineering hours.
- Latest concrete example: integrated iPhone replay completed 194 gameplay steps,
  defeated Sovereign and delivered five Flames at9HP/one life, opening the
  ending. Its failure was stale harness save readback after journal migration,
  not failed gameplay. Chromium was cancelled when Tayler redirected the work.

## Operating Rules

1. Maintain one short blocker list. Every item must identify a player-visible
   failure, a reproduction and an observable exit criterion.
2. Classify failures first: game defect, harness defect, strategy failure,
   infrastructure failure or physical-device limitation. Never weaken gameplay
   merely to make the bot win.
3. After two unsuccessful investigations of one reproduction, change the
   diagnostic method. Do not accumulate another nearly identical full replay.
4. Fix and test the smallest affected surface. Local placement is valid for
   reproducing a segment, but must never be presented as a complete playthrough.
5. Reuse prior evidence when the affected behavior is unchanged. Shared physics
   changes require focused geometry/physics regressions and representative
   browser checks, not automatic replay of every optional coin on both engines.
6. Keep lint, TypeScript, build/security budgets and final integrated tests as
   release gates. Run the full suite at integration checkpoints, not after
   each input-policy or documentation adjustment.
7. Delegate independent production slices with disjoint ownership. Do not create
   extra workers or infrastructure simply to maintain the checklist.
8. Finish the authorized new features, redesign, custom art and story before
   freezing the release candidate. These are not optional or deferred. Cut
   redundant verification and bot perfection, not product requirements.
9. Report completed product outcomes, remaining blockers and evidence limitations.
   Do not substitute test counts or polished source art for playable acceptance.

## Full Finish

Every chapter, mechanic, optional reward route and creative requirement still
gets reviewed. Normal progression needs credible playability evidence; mastery
needs reachable pickups/enemies and coherent scoring. Use targeted live segments,
physics/geometry tests, representative full runs, visual review and physical
player feedback together. A particular automated player completing every possible
browser/route combination is supplemental evidence, not the product definition.

Outstanding creative improvements and physical-phone checks must remain explicit
until completed. Do not declare a test release to be the finished rebuild.
No new guaranteed ETA is set by this reset. Update estimates only from the
bounded blocker list and actual results, not an indefinitely growing backlog.
