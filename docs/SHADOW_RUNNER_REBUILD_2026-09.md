## Initial Confirmed Findings

### R01: The Acceptance Suite Does Not Prove The Campaign

Priority: P1, release-blocking verification gap.
`scripts/shadow-runner-phone-smoke.mjs:837`, `:1102`, `:1490` restrict full
physics segments to Chromium. Calls such as `:1505` teleport checkpoints, and
finale assertions directly alter boss state. Existing tests can remain green
while an intervening jump, pickup, encounter or recovery path is broken.

Fix: preserve diagnostic shortcuts, but clearly separate them from input-only
route replays. Add deterministic route recording with normal health, normal
movement and no injected completion. Probe every surface-to-surface transition,
mandatory collectible and optional reward; failure must identify a location
and retain a screenshot/state trail.

### R02: Unrelated District Art Loads In The Tutorial

Priority: P1, phone startup and memory.
`createShadowRunnerPhaserGame.ts:540-595` unconditionally loads Ivy, Bell and
both Candle terrain atlases plus enemy types not present in early routes.
Tutorial capture confirms approximately 7.36 MiB across those four atlases.
The React image preloader and Phaser loading path also request common assets
separately. A new district should not make every previous district slower.

Fix: one dependency-driven level asset catalog; load only referenced terrain,
enemy, power and effect assets. Use WebP where its tested alpha/quality is
appropriate, retain originals outside public, and fail visibly with retry if
required art cannot load. Budgets test bytes and decoded dimensions.

### R03: Readability And Control Hierarchy Are Too Weak

Priority: P1 for game state, P2 for visual hierarchy.
`ShadowRunnerGame.tsx:214-242` renders health text at 0.38/0.44 rem; measured
phone text is 7.04 CSS px. The wide parchment HUD dominates the top while the
information inside it is tiny. Boss, player and ordinary enemy bars share a
similar red/gold treatment. Pause menu actions in `ShadowRunnerScrollMenu.tsx`
are 32/36px tall with tiny uppercase text. Important subtitles are shorter
than a reasonable reading interval and cannot be revisited in a journal.

Fix: compact readable health, lives, coins/total and score; enemy count and
mandatory objective progress; clearly different boss presentation; accessible
44px minimum menu commands (48px large mode); explicit crouch toggle with a
visible pressed state; uncluttered stable control zones and safe-area padding.
Readable sentence case, fixed type sizes, no viewport-scaled microtext.

### R04: The Environment Is A Static Picture, Not A Place

Priority: P2, core creative improvement.
`createShadowRunnerPhaserGame.ts:921` stretches one background to the same
screen, fixes its scroll factor to zero and overlays a dark shade. Scrolling
26,400 pixels does not reveal new spatial depth. Background bridges, stairs
and doorways often resemble real collision surfaces. The sprite-like hero and
some later painterly backgrounds differ noticeably in pixel density.

Fix: new art direction with restrained layered parallax, distant architecture
that cannot be confused with foreground surfaces, district landmarks placed
in world space, visible traversable upper edges, bounded ambient effects and
stateful checkpoint lights. Honor reduced motion live. Never substitute
procedural decorative platforms for the requested custom generated art.

### R05: Narrative And Replay Purpose Are Thin In Play

Priority: P2, campaign experience.
`SHADOW_RUNNER_STORY_LORE.md` still proposes different names and events for
levels 6-9 than the shipped catalog. The game shows a short intro for 2.8s
(`ShadowRunnerGame.tsx:554`) while gameplay begins, then largely relies on
objective counters. The finale has four text beats, but preceding discoveries
do not form an equally tangible chain of deliveries.

Fix: use the actual ten districts, with one short delivery briefing, two or
three earned environmental story beats and one recovered message per chapter.
A journal in pause and on the campaign screen preserves discovered story.
Finish with an earned relay-restoration sequence that can be skipped or
advanced manually, and optional lore acknowledgement without a withheld ending.

### R06: The Core Runtime Has Excessively Coupled Responsibilities

Priority: P2, reliability multiplier.
`createShadowRunnerPhaserGame.ts` is nearly 5,900 physical lines; rendering,
loading, inputs, combat, every power, UI callbacks, geometry and test shortcuts
share one scene. A level-number cascade governs asset dependencies. Broad
rewrites of all physics would be risky; leaving every concern tangled is also
costly for future fixes.

Fix: retain Phaser Arcade Physics and existing simulation contracts. Extract
well-tested movement/stance rules, render catalog, level validation, narrative
data and encounter presentation where they remove real complexity. Keep the
scene as orchestration, avoid a new engine or generic ECS rewrite.

## Chapter Direction

Each chapter uses teach, combine, test, release pacing. Safe checkpoint spaces
separate demands; long routes need resumable progress. Optional challenge lines
must visibly rejoin the route and never hide required completion behind a
one-time expiring power.

### Tutorial: The Courier Oath

- A sheltered outer courtyard; the sealed satchel is entrusted to the Runner.
- Learn move, jump, double jump, attack, crouch and recover in isolated rooms.
- Ghosted route marks and physical clearances teach through play, not a wall
  of control instructions. Explicit feedback when a required action succeeds.
- New readable training court art and quiet delivery pedestal.

### 1: East Gate Run

- Prove the first route still opens. The first revived relay sends an answer.
- Forgiving jumps progress into a bridge and a readable sentry duel.
- Distinguish the direct route from a high coin trail and a guarded cache.
- Moonlit pale limestone, silver tops, weathered purple oath banners.

### 2: Lantern Market Roofs

- The satchel contains names of missing couriers; stolen letters are for sale.
- Rooftop rhythm with visible landing zones, short vertical detours and a
  deliberate scout encounter rather than only more sentries and spikes.
- Lantern landmarks, roof materials distinct from background roofs.

### 3: Ivy Viaduct

- The road was sabotaged. An intact oath marker survives beside broken guards.
- Bridge balance is taught before barrels combine with it. Falling off must
  follow the visible surface, with readable salvage/recovery ledges.
- Wet moss, waterfall depth, broken spans and pale stone contact edges.

### 4: Bell Tower Archives

- Recover the forged lockdown order, not just reach an unexplained exit.
- True low-clearance passages, readable archer lanes and archive roof rewards.
- Bell mechanisms and stacked records make physical, solid obstacles; no arch
  illusion, invisible required stair or inaccessible under-platform coin.
- A brief safe archive reveal connects the Captain to the corrupted command.

### 5: Candle Fair Ruins

- The fair is transmitting decoy signals to prevent the city reconnecting.
- Telegraph multi-height volleys, teach shield timing, then combine with
  short-range thrown candles. Shelter pockets must interrupt every lane.
- Warm wax/fire versus cold stone, obvious safe surfaces, no fake required
  pickups. Difficult optional high paths remain readable.

### 6: Clockmaker Yard

- Restore the mechanism that carries messages between districts.
- Chrono timing, moving machinery and bandit pursuit; varied work bays rather
  than repeated pits. A recovery path remains when the power runs out.
- Iron, restrained brass, worklights and distinct moving-platform supports.

### 7: Moonlit Causeway

- The shards reconstruct the true command; the Rival appears ahead of us.
- Long-exposure crossings alternate with protected piers. Surge is an
  expressive route choice, never a mandatory pixel-perfect expiration race.
- Sea and moon-glass depth with noncollidable distant bridges clearly muted.

### 8: Courier Catacombs

- The courier dead preserved the route. The Rival learns the satchel is not
  treasure and leaves a fragment of the final warning.
- Wraithlight reveals optional passages; Mirror Ward counters guard pressure.
- Spectral art and solidity match. Sealed rooms remain escapable after death;
  every required seal is recoverable without restarting the entire chapter.
- Crypt jade, ivory route edges and weathered courier seals.

### 9: Captain Gate

- The Captain explains the lockdown: the signal itself carried the lie.
- Wind shelters, lifts and shield-breaking create a three-part fortress
  approach, followed by a duel with readable attack/recovery states.
- Watchfire crests mark progression; recovered orders show why he stayed.
- Storm-lit towers, red watch banners and bright windbreak silhouettes.

### 10: Dawn Relay Spire

- Deliver the true order. Earlier chapter motifs return with a purpose.
- Distinct approach, foundry, choir, ascent and crown acts; checkpoints at
  meaningful transitions and before the boss. Remove repeated filler without
  shrinking the finale's scope or challenge.
- Phase bridges and relay beams use visual anticipation and dependable cover.
- Sovereign duel: identifiable phase tells, punish windows, limited overlapping
  threats, deterministic recoverability after death and no exhausted-power trap.
- The player lights the relay after victory. Light travels outward through the
  districts, the city answers, the sealed message is delivered. The Rival and
  Captain receive a short resolution. The full ending belongs to every winner.
