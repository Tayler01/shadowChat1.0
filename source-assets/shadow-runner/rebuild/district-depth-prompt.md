# District Depth Atlas

Built-in ImageGen, 2026-09-30. Genuine generated alpha; eight cells. Masters remain outside runtime. The first seven cells are chapter depth art and the eighth is preserved for reuse.

Use case: sprite asset sheet for Shadow Runner gothic 2D platformer. Genuine transparent background, no checkerboard. A precisely regular 4-column by 2-row atlas of EIGHT isolated hanging architectural props, one prop centered entirely inside each equal square cell, ample transparent margins, no object crosses a cell edge, no ground or floor, no text labels, no borders. Pixel-painterly finely detailed medieval game art, pale silver stone, dark iron, gold and crimson accents, readable clean silhouette, frontal sideview-compatible, no perspective ground. Every prop hangs downward from top with recognizable architecture, not walkable platforms. Row1 left to right: 1 East Gate pale limestone hanging arch ornament with small red pennant; 2 Lantern Market hanging iron lantern under weathered timber bracket; 3 Ivy Viaduct silver stone suspended arch fragment with trailing ivy; 4 Bell Tower hanging bronze bell with stone corbel and archival chain. Row2 left to right: 5 Candle Fair old festival iron chandelier with amber candles and crimson ribbons; 6 Clockmaker Yard large suspended brass cog assembly with dark iron chains; 7 Moonlit Causeway moon-glass hanging crystalline lantern with silver iron cage and pale cyan light; 8 small weathered courier iron sign with tiny sealed scroll motif, no letters. Consistent detailed custom gothic fantasy game materials matching hooded courier environments, not photorealistic or 3D renders. All objects separated, fully visible and unclipped, transparent background everywhere except props. Overall landscape 2:1 canvas, 4x2 equal square cells.
