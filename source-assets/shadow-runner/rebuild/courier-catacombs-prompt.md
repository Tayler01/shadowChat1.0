# Courier Catacombs Background

Built-in ImageGen. New opaque background; preserve the legacy art separately.
Gameplay platforms and hanging lantern depth objects are not baked into this layer.

## Exact Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Production raster background panorama for Shadow Runner chapter Courier Catacombs, 16:9 opaque full bleed, coherent hand-painted pixel art with deliberate crisp pixel clusters, richly crafted Gothic stone details matching a hooded courier platform game. A vast underground courier necropolis seen across a deep dark central void. Recurring distant stone vault bays, tall narrow sealed dispatch niches, bronze conduit pipes and chain mechanisms, deeply recessed Gothic archways receding in layered cool gray/green air. The central middle distance contains a small closed bronze relay vault with a weathered wing-shaped seal, flanked by narrow muted wine-red cloth banners. Turquoise light shines faintly from glass windows deep in the walls; a few sparse warm amber niches contrast with cold silver-gray stone. Extremely atmospheric but sharp, not blurry, not photorealistic. All architecture and the closed vault are FAR beyond the playable plane, with low contrast and no foreground floor, no nearest walkways, no fake jump platforms, no horizontal bridge in front, no characters, no skull closeups, no gore, no UI, no lettering. Lower third: distant vertical shafts and receding vertical masonry descending into darkness, no grounded floor plane. Upper third: monumental vaulted ceiling fading into shadow. No large hanging foreground lanterns: those will be a separately generated transparent depth layer. Preserve dark-medieval mystery, long-lost deliveries and the original cyan/bronze vault identity while making true silver gameplay surfaces and golden pickups visually distinct. Not a one-note purple or brown scene. No lens bloom, bokeh, blur, decorative orbs or camera effects.
