# Courier Catacombs Asset Prompts

These prompts produced the original Level 8 source art on July 27, 2026.
Runtime files are deterministic crops and resizes created by
`scripts/process-shadow-runner-level8-assets.mjs`.

## Shared Direction

Premium Shadow Runner pixel art with charcoal-green catacomb stone, pale
limestone collision edges, oxidized copper machinery, parchment gold,
burgundy courier marks, warm amber lamps, and restrained spectral cyan-jade.
All gameplay silhouettes must remain readable at the 740 by 390 phone profile.

## Background

An enormous buried courier-relay hall in exact 16:9, with a central sealed
relay door, old message vaults, chains, amber lamps, burgundy banners, copper
machinery, spectral message glass, and a visually quiet lower gameplay band.
No text, characters, UI, or baked foreground platforms.

## Terrain And Props

Side-view platformer atlas on flat `#00ff00`: extra-wide floor, cracked rubble
floor, medium ledge, chained bridge, recovery slab, low overhang, spectral
bridge, hidden wall, relay door, seal altar, cache pedestal, and chain anchor.
Every piece isolated with generous gutters and a bright, level walkable edge.

## Tomb Lurker

Six right-facing frames on flat `#ff00ff`: dormant marker, warning rune,
emergence, lunge, hit, and defeated rubble. Broken charcoal stone, pale mask,
burgundy dispatch cloth, copper buckles, coral eye, and jade spirit glow.

## Crypt Warden

Six right-facing frames on flat `#ff00ff`: idle, walk, brace, polearm charge,
hit, and defeated. Massive pale stone armor, charcoal joints, copper trim,
burgundy burial sash, jade lantern shield, and coral eye slit.

## Rival Courier

Six right-facing frames on flat `#ff00ff`: ready, run, dash, hooked-blade
attack, hit, and defeated. Lean runner, ivory half-mask, charcoal leather,
copper courier hardware, pale half-cape, crimson route sash, and jade dispatch
tube.

## Wraithlight Lantern

Four pulse frames on flat `#ff00ff`: compact copper courier lantern with a pale
stone cap, burgundy ribbon, and cyan-jade rune glass.

## Mirror Ward

Four reflection frames on flat `#ff00ff`: floating kite-shaped silvered mirror
shield with copper-gold frame, burgundy grip ribbon, jade reflection rune, and
a final deflection spark.

## Relay Seal

Four rotation frames on flat `#ff00ff`: major circular courier authority seal
with gold-copper ring, burgundy ribbon tails, messenger-wing rune, and jade
message-glass core.

## Courier Cache

Four reveal and opening frames on flat `#ff00ff`: rare dispatch chest with
charcoal stone body, pale lid edge, copper hardware, burgundy seal, and glowing
spectral message scrolls.

## Campaign Button

Edit the established Level 8 map button while preserving its gothic frame and
transparent silhouette. Replace the inner art with the Courier Catacombs relay
door identity and set the destination label to `CATACOMBS`.
