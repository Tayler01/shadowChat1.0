# Captain Gate Asset Prompts

These prompts produced the original Level 9 source art on July 27, 2026.
Runtime files are deterministic crops and resizes created by
`scripts/process-shadow-runner-level9-assets.mjs`.

## Shared Direction

Premium Shadow Runner pixel art with pale silver limestone, charcoal masonry,
blue-black iron, oxidized copper, restrained crimson command cloth, warm amber
watchfires, teal storm energy, and pale dawn gold. Gameplay silhouettes and
collision edges must remain readable at the 740 by 390 phone profile.

## Background

Exact 16:9 side-view cliffside fortress before dawn with Captain Gate,
watchtowers, counterweight chains, signal braziers, crimson banners, sea
cliffs, storm clouds, and a visually quiet lower gameplay band. No text,
characters, UI, or baked foreground platforms.

## Terrain And Props

Side-view platformer atlas on flat `#00ff00`: extra-wide battlement floor,
cracked floor, medium ledge, counterweight bridge, recovery step, low
overhang, chain lift, breakable barricade, Captain Gate portcullis, watchfire
pedestal, command chest, and windbreak anchor. Every piece is isolated with
large gutters and a bright walkable edge.

## Gate Pikeman

Six right-facing frames on flat `#ff00ff`: guarded idle, walk, shield brace,
long pike thrust, guard-broken hit, and defeated. Pale plate, blue-black mail,
charcoal tower shield, copper pike, crimson sash, amber eye slit, and a teal
storm-glass buckle.

## Storm Grenadier

Six right-facing frames on flat `#ff00ff`: alert idle, walk, low wind-up,
arcing bomb throw, hit, and defeated. Blue-black padded armor, pale shoulders,
copper mask and harness, crimson belt, amber goggles, and teal-fused iron
bombs.

## Moonlit Captain

Eight right-facing frames on flat `#ff00ff`: command idle, advance, shield
brace, shoulder charge, sword sweep, storm command, heavy hit, and defeated.
Crested pale-steel helmet, blue-black armor, crimson command cape, copper trim,
round storm shield, pale-gold blade, and restrained teal lightning.

## Gale Mantle

Four pulse frames on flat `#ff00ff`: compact blue-black enchanted mantle with
silver feather clasps, crimson lining, copper chain, teal wind runes, and a
pale-gold edge.

## Sunsteel Edge

Four charge frames on flat `#ff00ff`: floating pale-gold short sword and crest
with charcoal grip, copper guard, crimson ribbon, amber core, teal
counter-runes, and a final guard-breaking slash pulse.

## Watchfire Crest

Four rotation and ignition frames on flat `#ff00ff`: circular pale-stone and
copper authority crest with a portcullis rune, crimson ribbon, amber watchfire
core, blue-black rim, and teal studs.

## Captain's Orders

Four reveal frames on flat `#ff00ff`: rare blue-black command case with pale
edge, copper hardware, crimson seal, parchment orders, amber glow, and a teal
storm cipher. No readable writing.

## Storm Bomb

Four projectile and impact frames on flat `#ff00ff`: dormant iron bomb,
airborne spin, target marker, and compact teal-and-amber electrical burst.

## Campaign Button

Edit the established Level 9 map button while preserving its gothic frame,
transparent silhouette, number medallion, and label plaque. Replace the inner
art with the Captain Gate stormwatch identity and set the label to
`CAPTAIN GATE`.
