# Dawn Relay Spire Asset Prompts

These prompts produced the original Level 10 source art on July 28, 2026.
Runtime files are deterministic crops and resizes created by
`scripts/process-shadow-runner-level10-assets.mjs`.

All images were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Transparent game
assets were generated on flat chroma-key backgrounds and converted to alpha
with the installed ImageGen chroma removal helper.

## Shared Direction

Premium Shadow Runner pixel art with pale silver-white stone, charcoal
masonry, blue-black iron, blackened brass, oxidized copper, restrained crimson
cloth, amber fire, cyan moon-glass energy, and pale dawn gold. Gameplay
silhouettes and collision edges must remain readable at the 740 by 390 phone
profile.

## Background

Exact 16:9 side-view ancient relay tower above the clouds before sunrise, with
moon-glass machinery, giant clockwork rings, beacon mirrors, high bridges,
cables, braziers, distant towers, and first light at the horizon. The lower 28
percent remains visually quiet. No text, characters, UI, or baked foreground
platforms.

## Terrain And Machinery

Side-view platformer atlas on flat `#00ff00`: extra-wide relay floor, cracked
floor, moon-glass ledge, phase bridge, recovery stair, low conduit overhang,
counterweight lift, cover pylon, master-lock gate, relay flame brazier,
ignition pedestal, and relay-beam emitter. Every piece is isolated with large
gutters and a bright walkable edge.

## Relay Lancer

Six right-facing frames on flat `#ff00ff`: guarded idle, mechanical march,
high lance sweep, grounded charge, guard-broken hit, and defeated collapse.
Pale plate, blue-black machinery, blackened brass joints, cyan moon-glass
lance, crimson ribbons, dawn-gold eye slit, and a relay core.

## Prism Caster

Six right-facing frames on flat `#ff00ff`: alert idle, walk, relay-orb charge,
orb release, hit, and defeated. Faceted pale-metal prism mask, blue-black robe
armor, mechanical arms, copper trim, crimson sash, cyan shoulder prisms, and a
dawn-gold relay core.

## Gearwing Drone

Six right-facing frames on flat `#ff00ff`: compact hover, wings extended,
dive warning, diagonal attack, stunned spin, and broken fall. Silver blade
wings, blue-black body armor, brass gears, copper joints, cyan eye and core,
crimson tail ribbons, and dawn-gold warning fins.

## Sentry Sovereign

Ten right-facing frames in two rows on flat `#ff00ff`: command idle, armored
advance, shield brace, high halberd sweep, relay-orb cast, shoulder charge,
ground shockwave, exposed-core stagger, Last Light enrage, and defeated
collapse. Monumental silver-white plate, blue-black machinery, brass skeleton,
copper trim, crimson mantle, moon-glass halberd, master-lock shield, cyan relay
energy, and a dawn-gold heart core.

## Relay Orb

Four frames on flat `#ff00ff`: dormant moon-glass orb, charged airborne spin,

## Grand Ending

Exact 16:9 cinematic victory scene of the restored Dawn Relay Spire at sunrise.
The Last Runner stands at the ignition pedestal as five relay flames ignite and
golden beams connect distant towers across the clouds. The moon fades on the
left, dawn rises on the right, and the shattered master lock rests in the
foreground. No text or UI.

## Campaign Button

The established Level 10 campaign frame, number medallion, and plaque are
preserved. The circular interior is rebuilt from the generated Dawn Relay
Spire background so the marker uses new level art while remaining consistent
with the campaign map.
